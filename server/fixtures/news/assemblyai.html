#Universal-3.6 Pro Realtime: Streaming Speech-to-Text for Voice Agents Across 32 Languages

Universal-3.6 Pro Realtime is now live. It is a direct upgrade to Universal-3.5 Pro Realtime, trained on tens of thousands of hours of real voice-agent and telephony conversations, so the accuracy you get on quiet calls…

Universal-3.6 Pro Realtime is now live. It is a direct upgrade to Universal-3.5 Pro Realtime, trained on tens of thousands of hours of real voice-agent and telephony conversations, so the accuracy you get on quiet calls now holds on loud rooms, accents, and phone lines. It serves 32 languages from one model with automatic language detection, at the same latency and the same $0.45/hr as Universal-3.5 Pro.

  • Short answers, heard right. On our English voice-agent benchmark, one-word replies like "no" and "yeah" come back wrong 1.45% of the time, down from 2.65% on Universal-3.5 Pro, with the largest gain in heavy background noise.
  • Background voices stay out of the transcript. On the same benchmark, words picked up from a television or a nearby coworker fall from 3.53 to 2.52 per 1,000 words. Optional voice focus, off by default, suppresses other talkers farther from the microphone than the caller.
  • 32 languages. Fourteen are new: Korean, Catalan, Galician, Russian, Romanian, Persian, Estonian, Afrikaans, Marathi, Zulu, Urdu, Xhosa, Cantonese, and Norwegian Nynorsk. Speech that switches language mid-sentence is transcribed without pinning a language.
  • Entity-aware endpointing. The turn stays open while a caller is reading out a number, code, email, or address. On the same benchmark, end-of-turn precision rises from 74.1% to 77.9%, and median endpoint latency is unchanged at 537 ms.

How to use it

Add speech_model=universal-3-6-pro to your existing streaming connection parameters. There is no new endpoint, and every parameter you use today works the same way. Universal-3.5 Pro Realtime stays available, so you can run both against the same calls before you switch.
